## Welcome to CityStops!
An app whose purpose is for users to create, plan, and share daytrip itineraries in their local city.

## Features

In this app, the user can:
- **Create** a Daytrip route by choosing locations on a map 
- **Publish** your route on the app for other users to see 
- **View** other people’s routes on a feed 
- **Save** other people’s routes 
- **Receive personalised Trip suggestions** using Machine Learning 
- Create a route using **Dora, our AI model**
